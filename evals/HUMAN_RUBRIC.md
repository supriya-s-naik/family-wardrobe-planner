# Human review rubric

Use this rubric for subjective qualities that deterministic checks cannot establish. Reviewers score each category from 1 (poor) to 5 (excellent) and add one sentence of evidence.

| Category | A score of 5 means |
|---|---|
| Event fit | Every outfit clearly matches the event, setting, and dress code. |
| Weather readiness | Layers, footwear, and rain protection suit the supplied forecast and activities. |
| Personal comfort | Recommendations visibly respect each family member's fit, movement, and comfort needs. |
| Family coordination | Colors and formality feel coordinated without making the outfits identical. |
| Grounded rationale | Explanations connect selected item IDs to structured data and cited guidance. |
| Purchase value | Each proposed purchase fills a real gap and has credible reuse value. |

Record the reviewer, date, case ID, six scores, evidence notes, and overall comments. Human scores are reported separately from the automated pass rate.
